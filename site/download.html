Free, on your laptop and on your phone.

Windows

Winston floats over whatever you are doing, always on top, so he is there when you are in Brightspace, Google Docs or anywhere else.

Download
The installer starts downloading right away. If Windows warns about an unknown publisher, choose More info, then Run anyway.

Mac

The same floating companion for your Mac, with a small menu bar icon to hide and show him.

Download
For Macs with Apple silicon (2020 and newer). The first time you open it, right click the app and choose Open.

iPhone

No App Store needed. Open Winston in Safari and add him to your home screen, and he opens full screen like any other app.

Open Winston
Android works too: open it in Chrome and tap Install.

Putting Winston On Your iPhone

1
Open the Winston web app in Safari.
2
Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen.
3
Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then tap Add.
4
Open Winston from your home screen. Every time I improve him, he updates by himself.

The desktop app always opens the newest version of Winston, so you will never have to download it again to get new features. A phone cannot float him over other apps, which is a limit of the phone rather than of Winston, so for class I suggest Class Mode on your phone and the floating Winston on your laptop.
