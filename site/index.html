Why I Made Him

There is a certain kind of loneliness that comes with studying. You sit in a library or at a kitchen table, the pages blur together, and the whole world seems to ask more of you than you have to give. Anyone who has ever stared at a syllabus at midnight knows it well.

Study Paws was made for that exact feeling. Your pet is a small, warm presence who sits beside you in class, takes the notes you cannot keep up with, and gently reminds you that you belong in the room. Your pet is part study tool and part companion, because I have always believed that people learn best when they feel supported by everything around them.

The idea is simple. Learning should feel like a place you want to return to, a little like a town where everyone knows your name and every small win is celebrated. Study Paws is my attempt to build that place, one pet and one study session at a time.
