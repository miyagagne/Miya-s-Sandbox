Features

Built around the way students really feel.

Windows Mac iPhone

Class, Made Calmer

Live Notes

Class moves quickly, and the instinct is to write everything down and absorb nothing. Winston listens alongside you and gathers what matters, so the lecture becomes something you take part in rather than race against. Some professors do not allow recording, and I understand that completely, so there is a Typing Mode that keeps the microphone off and organizes the notes you write yourself.

Summaries You Can Actually Use

When class ends, you get a bullet point recap of what the teacher covered, with the dates and to-dos pulled out on their own. You can edit every word of it, because notes should always feel like yours.

Ask Winston Anything

He goes to class with you, so he knows what has been said. Ask what is happening in a class this week, what is due soon, or for a topic to be explained one gentle step at a time.

Practice And Progress

Questions From Your Own Notes

Practice questions come straight from your materials, which means you are always reviewing what your class actually covers. Right answers earn coins, and mistakes turn into short explanations instead of stress.

Quests, Streaks And Levels

Small daily goals build a streak, and the streak builds a habit. Watching your level climb makes progress feel visible, which is often what keeps us going on the hard days.

Study Cards And Class Hubs

Every class has its own hub for notes, materials, a calendar and a study timer. Upload a syllabus and Winston finds the dates for you.

Feeling Good While You Study

The Boost Hub

Bored, stressed or unmotivated, every feeling gets an answer: a breathing exercise, a quick game, or a pep talk that is honest rather than cheesy. Learning is as emotional as it is intellectual, and Winston treats it that way.

Class Mode And The High Paw

Keep Winston open on your phone and he stays beside you for the whole class, checking in with a gentle high paw now and then. You can make it rarer, or turn it off completely, because attention should never feel like a chore.

Light And Dark

A bright farm by day and a quiet night barn after dark, so studying feels comfortable at any hour.

Together

Friends By Username

Search for a friend, send a request, and the two of you appear on one another's boards. It is a small thing, but studying beside someone, even from a distance, changes how it feels.

Leaderboards Near And Far

Compare study time with your friends, your school, your state or your country. Join your school and see where your own campus stands.

The Dog Park

A shared space for you and your friends to take a break, play fetch and wave hello between sessions. Rest is part of learning too.

Yours To Keep

Pick Your Pet

Choose from four dogs and four cats, then give your companion a name of your own. Dress them in hats and glasses you earn with the coins from your studying.

Your Account, Your Password

Create an account with an email or with Google, protect it with a password, and your pet, classes and notes follow you from device to device.

Google Classroom And Brightspace

Connect either one and your classes, due dates and materials arrive on their own. Brightspace works through a quick button you click in your own signed in tab, so your password never leaves your school.

On Every Screen

Install Winston on your laptop and he floats over whatever you are working on. On your phone, add him to your home screen and he opens like any other app.
